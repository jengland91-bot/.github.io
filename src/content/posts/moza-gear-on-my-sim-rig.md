I co-drive trophy trucks in the real dirt and I also sit in a cockpit in the shop. The sim side has to feel like a tool, not a toy, or I will not use it.

## What I tell people to buy first

If you are coming from a Logitech or a first-gen Thrustmaster, get the **Moza R5 bundle**. It is the daily driver on my rig. The **R3 bundle** is the honest budget step if you want direct drive without going all-in on day one.

I also keep a partner code on the [Codes](/partners) page if you would rather buy from Moza direct: **JENGLAND91**.

## What stays on the rig

- **KS wheel** when I want a round rim.
- **CS V2P** when the car is pointy and I want a smaller diameter.
- **CM2 dash** so I am not covering the game with twenty overlays.
- **HBP handbrake** and **SGP sequential** for rally, drift, and anything that should not live on paddles.

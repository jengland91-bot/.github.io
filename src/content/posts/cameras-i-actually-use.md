Professional jobs still go through [Rise Above Photo](https://riseabovephoto.com). This list is the creator kit — the cameras that come to the shop, the desert, and a sit-down stream.

## Handheld

**DJI Osmo Pocket 3** is the one-camera answer. Gimbal in the handle, good enough for interviews and B-roll, small enough that people do not freeze up.

## Mounted and dirty

**Osmo Action 5 Pro** gets bolted to the truck or the rig. If it might get dust, vibration, or a roll cage in the shot, that is the body.

## When I do not know the frame yet

**Insta360 X5** (and Ace Pro 2 as a second angle) is how I cover a space and decide the crop in the edit. Socials want 9:16, YouTube wants 16:9, and I am usually one person. Shoot 360, reframe later.

If you are buying Insta360 direct, use the code on [Codes](/partners) — **INRS5VV**.

## Audio

The **FIFINE USB/XLR** stays on the desk. USB when I am traveling, XLR when it is parked. Garage fans lose to a dynamic mic more often than a condenser.

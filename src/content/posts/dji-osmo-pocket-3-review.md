The Pocket 3 is the camera that made me stop packing a “maybe” body for walk-around days. Gimbal in the handle, a screen that flips for the subject, and it does not look like I showed up with a film crew.

## Who it is for

People who need clean talking-head and B-roll without a second operator. Creators who shoot 16:9 and 9:16 in the same afternoon. Anyone who is tired of a phone looking like a phone.

## Who should skip it

If the camera is going on a roll cage, a bumper, or anything that will eat dust, get the [Action 5 Pro](/gear/dji-osmo-action-5-pro) instead. The Pocket 3 is a people camera. It is not a bolt-on.

## What I like

- Face tracking that I do not have to babysit
- Small enough that interviews stay human
- Files that grade without a fight

## What I do not

- Wind on the built-in mics — I still run the FIFINE or a wireless when it matters
- I will not mount this on the truck

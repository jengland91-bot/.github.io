TITLE
Steering Wheel Mount compatible with MOZA R3 R5 R9 | Wall or Sim Rig QR Hanger

TAGS
sim racing, steering wheel mount, wheel hanger, wall mount, QR mount, 8020, garage storage, racing wheel, sim rig, PETG

DESCRIPTION
Hang your sim-racing wheel on the wall (or your rig) instead of leaving it on a chair.

This is a 3D-printed quick-release hanger. Line up the wheel the same way you do on the base and press on — six ball seats stop it spinning if you bump it. Pull the QR collar to take it off.

Fits:
• MOZA Racing wheels with the stock ball-lock QR (ES, RS, CS, GS, FSR, and others)
• This hangs the WHEEL, not the wheelbase
• Other D1-spec / 50 mm ball-lock QRs (including many Simagic wheels)

You get:
• 1× round mount, printed in PETG
• 1× M8 through the middle of the stub (drop the bolt in from the front; hidden by the wheel)
• 4× countersunk #8 holes around the rim for a wall
• Screws / T-nuts not included unless you add the hardware option

Use it on a wall or on your rig — same part. Install into a wall stud or use rated anchors. Do not hang a wheel on a single drywall screw.

Matching 8020 add-ons (same M8 / 40 mm T-nuts): phone holder, cup holder, headphone hook, cable clip, mouse tray. See the kit listing.

# Bambu Lab P2S — print guide

## Recommended kits to sell

1. **Evolve / tubular kit** — `seat_cradle` + `clamp_40mm_front` + `clamp_40mm_rear`  
2. **Aluminum-profile kit** — `seat_cradle` + `adapter_4040` (+ M8 T-nut & bolt)  
3. **Desk kit** — `seat_cradle` + `desk_stand_base`  

## Filament

| Part | Material | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| Seat cradle | PETG (preferred) or PLA+ | PETG survives cockpit heat / sun better |
| Clamp halves | PETG | Clamp loads + creep resistance |
| Desk base | PLA+ or PETG | Looks fine either way |

Colors that sell: satin black shell, optional contrasting “cushion” paint or dual-color AMS if you split the model later. Avoid glossy candy colors for a “race parts” look.

## Bambu Studio profile (starting point)

- **Layer height:** 0.20 mm (0.16 mm for hero photos)  
- **Nozzle:** 0.4 mm  
- **Walls:** 4  
- **Infill:** 25% gyroid (clamp: 40%)  
- **Top/bottom:** 5  
- **Supports:**  
  - Seat: tree supports on dovetail underside if printed upright; or print reclined on the back with supports in the pocket  
  - Clamp: print split-face on bed → usually **no supports**  
- **Brim:** 5 mm on seat if bed adhesion is fussy  
- **Ironing:** optional on seat outer for product photos  

### Orientation

- **Seat:** upright (dovetail pointing back) OR on its back for strongest phone rails. Upright looks cleaner; on-back is stronger.  
- **Clamp halves:** flat on the split face.  
- **4040 adapter:** plate flat on bed.  
- **Desk base:** base flat on bed.

## Fit checklist before you list on Etsy

1. Measure your Evolve tube with calipers (expect ~40.0 mm). If powder coat is thick, you may want `tube_od = 40.6` in the generator.  
2. Test phones: SE / standard / Pro Max **with cases**. Pocket is ~88 × 16.8 mm.  
3. Confirm dovetail slides in and locks without cracking — sand lightly if tight.  
4. Torque M5 clamp bolts snug; do not crush the tube paint (use rubber shim).

## Photo tips (conversion)

- On the real Evolve 3.0, clamp to the **wheel arch upright** or a side tube within reach.  
- Phone showing iRacing / CrewChief / Spotify.  
- One lifestyle shot + one clean white-bg product shot + one hardware/scale shot.

# Next steps to a sellable product

1. **Print a prototype** on the P2S using `docs/PRINT_GUIDE_P2S.md`.
2. **Caliper-check** Evolve 3.0 tube OD and your phone + case; tweak `scripts/generate_stl_v2.py` and regenerate.
3. **Refine look** in OpenSCAD / Bambu Studio / Blender once you have a physical in hand (fillets, cushion split for AMS, logo-free badge).
4. **Photograph** on the real Evolve 3.0.
5. **List** with `docs/ETSY_LISTING_DRAFT.md` — physical kits first.
6. Optional: add a product page under the blog later (`src/content/gear/`) once you have an Amazon or Etsy URL.

## Design differentiators already baked in

- Open shoulder / no head wings (distinct from common Recaro-style remixes)
- 40 mm tubular clamp (matches Evolve 3.0) — market gap vs aluminum-only free models
- Modular dovetail: same cradle, three mounts
- Charge cable notch
- Decorative harness piercings (original styling cue)

## Short blurb

Original open-shoulder racing-bucket phone cradle inspired by EVO-class seats. Fits iPhone up through 18 Pro Max with case, charge hole in the bottom, and swap-in backs: vertical rail, horizontal rail, desk legs, or an M3 kick-out for desk / 40 mm tube clamps.

## Description bullets

- Original design (not a free-model remix)  
- Open-shoulder EVO-style silhouette — no tall head wings  
- Pocket sized for iPhone 18 Pro Max + thick case (92 × 18 mm)  
- Charge notch + cable drop at the bottom  
- Modular M3 backs: vertical rail, horizontal rail, desk, DIY  
- Kick-out accepts M3 bolt for desk clamp or round tube clamp  
- Printed in PETG on Bambu Lab P2S  

## What to say about “looks like the MakerWorld one”

You can say it’s in the same *product category* (mini race seat that holds a phone) but is an original shell with a modular mount system. Don’t upload or sell their STL.

## Full description (paste & edit)

**Open-Shoulder Bucket Phone Cradle**

A mini racing bucket that actually holds your phone.

Most “race seat” phone stands on free sites are the same Recaro-style remix. This one is an **original open-shoulder** design — low crown, no tall head wings — closer to modern fixed-back buckets, with a clean charge notch and a modular mount.

### What’s included (choose your variant)

- Phone cradle (fits phones ~88 mm wide × ~16 mm thick with slim/medium cases)  
- **Tubular kit:** 2-piece 40 mm clamp + cradle  
- **Profile kit:** 4040/4080 M8 adapter + cradle  
- **Desk kit:** weighted-look stand + cradle  

Hardware: tubular kit uses 2× M5×30 screws + locknuts (included / not included — set your policy).

### Fits

- 40 mm steel-tube sim cockpits (Evolve-style frames and similar)  
- 4040 / 4080 aluminum profile with M8 T-nut (adapter version)  
- Desk / shelf (stand version)

### Print / finish

Printed in PETG on a Bambu Lab P2S. Light layer lines are normal for FDM; we can offer sanded / primed finishes as an upgrade.

### Notes

- Not affiliated with Sparco, Recaro, or any cockpit brand.  
- Measure your phone with its case. Custom pocket sizes available on request.  
- Do not overtighten the tube clamp on painted frames — a thin rubber shim is recommended.

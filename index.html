3D print · Stream Deck Plus · 6 Sigma

Ring on the outside. Back plate on the rig.

A picture-frame ring that only wraps the outside of the Stream Deck Plus — nothing over the keys or dials — screwed to a back plate that bolts onto your 6 Sigma rail with the same M8 T-nuts the chassis already uses.

Download

front_ring.stlOuter frame only. Open window for the whole face. back_plate.stlPlate + two slotted M8 holes for T-nuts. ring-1to1.svgPaper template. Print at 100% scale. params.pyLip, size, hole spacing — edit and regenerate.

Hardware

QtyPart
4M3 × 20 socket cap — through the back plate into the ring posts
2M8 × 16 + M8 drop-in T-nuts (8 mm slot) — already in a 6 Sigma kit

Print

PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 30–40% gyroid, no supports. Ring: frame on the bed. Back plate: M8 pad on the bed.

Put it together

  1. Print the SVG at 100%. The white window must leave every key and dial uncovered.
  2. Remove the Plus stand (two Phillips screws).
  3. Sit the Plus on the back plate, USB-C in the notch.
  4. Drop the ring over the outside of the face.
  5. Four M3s from the back into the ring corners.
  6. Two M8s through the pad into T-nuts on a 40 mm face — shifter arm, wheel-deck upright, or a spare 4040.

If the ring is tight, raise CLEAR or FACE_H in cad/params.py and run python3 cad/generate.py. Full notes in the README.

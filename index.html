Ring, back plate, hinge.

Built around your measured Plus: 139.6 × 135.0 × 29.9 mm. Four screw corners and thin straps on the front, pockets cut out of the back, and a cable gate so the USB-C charger drops through. The back plate hinges on a short 4040 clip so you can hang it on a vertical upright, tilt it, and lock the angle.

Print

PETG, 0.20 mm, 4 walls, 40% gyroid, no supports. Ring: frame on the bed. Back plate: Plus-face on the bed (hinge sticks up). Clamp: back wall on the bed.

Put it together

  1. Print the SVG at 100%. Window must clear the keys and dials.
  2. Stand off the Plus. Sit it on the back plate. USB-C drops in through the logo-end gate.
  3. Ring over the outside. Four M3s from the back.
  4. Clip the short U onto the vertical 4040 upright. One M8 into a T-nut on the far face.
  5. M5 through the three ears. Aim the face, snug the nylock. That’s the adjustable angle.

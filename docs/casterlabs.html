Caffeinated owns on-stream chat, alerts, labels, and goals — plus the docks only you see. Keep it running on the same PC as OBS. Official docs: widgets, docks.

Accounts

Settings → Accounts: connect every platform you stream to. Use combined widgets (all platforms) unless you want separate art per site.

YouTube: channel subscribers = Followers in Caffeinated. Channel members = Subscribers.

Chat behavior

  • Bottom-up
  • LIVE: messages disappear after ~20 seconds
  • JUST CHATTING: longer or persistent
  • Platform icons on
  • Follow/sub/raid events in chat off if the alert widget already shows them

Docks (not on stream)

OBS → Docks → Custom Browser Docks. Paste Chat, Activity Feed, Viewer List from Caffeinated’s Docks tab.

References

Build widgets once in LIVE, then Paste (Reference) into JUST CHATTING and only change the transform. Do not also play the same alert in Lumia on screen.

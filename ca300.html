California 300

Install like Parker 400: download the ZIP and put it in your mods folder. Do not extract.

Download california_300.zip
  1. Download california_300.zip
  2. Do not extract it
  3. Press Win+R, paste this, Enter:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods
  4. Put california_300.zip in that mods folder
  5. Open BeamNG → Freeroam → California 300

Includes CA300 Race Ready course, pit row, Start/Finish spawn, and dirt materials.
